PERMIAN  ->  save as art/sheets/gems-permian.png

Use the attached image as the EXACT style reference. It shows 18 finished gems from my match-3 game. Make 6 NEW gems in precisely the same style for the PERMIAN era (299 to 252 million years ago).

LAYOUT (very important):
- One landscape image, 3:2 ratio, flat solid dark navy background #0F1424 everywhere, no gradient, no texture, no vignette.
- A grid of 3 columns x 2 rows, 6 gems total, evenly spaced. Each gem is centred in its own cell and fills about 75% of the cell.
- Wide empty gaps of plain background between gems. Gems, glows and sparkles must NEVER touch or overlap each other or the image edge.
- Top row, left to right: red, green, blue. Bottom row, left to right: yellow, purple, amber.

GEM SHAPES AND COLOURS (copy them exactly from the reference, same column = same shape and colour):
1. Red OCTAGON, ruby red.
2. Green upright OVAL, emerald green.
3. Blue DIAMOND / rhombus standing on a point, sapphire blue.
4. Yellow TRIANGLE pointing DOWN, golden yellow.
5. Purple PENTAGON with a row of small crystal spikes along its top edges, amethyst purple.
6. Amber TEARDROP with the point at the top, orange amber, translucent.

STYLE (match the reference exactly):
- Glossy faceted gemstone, crisp facet planes, a thick dark outline around the whole gem, a soft inner glow, light from the top left, a few small white star sparkles on the edges.
- In the centre of each gem, one bold engraved EMBLEM as a clean single-colour silhouette in a darker tone of the gem's own colour, slightly recessed, lower contrast than the gem outline, readable at 48 pixels. Side view where it is an animal. No fine hair-thin detail.
- Same size, same outline weight and same lighting on all 6 gems. Each gem must look like it belongs in the same set as the reference.

EMBLEMS for the Permian era:
1. Red octagon: Inostrancevia, a big sabre-toothed gorgonopsian predator walking, long fangs showing
2. Green oval: a single Glossopteris leaf, long tongue-shaped leaf with a central vein
3. Blue diamond: Dimetrodon walking, with its tall spined sail on its back
4. Yellow triangle: the spiral tooth-whorl of Helicoprion, a coiled spiral of triangular teeth
5. Purple pentagon: Scutosaurus, a stocky armoured plant-eater with a bumpy head
6. Amber teardrop: a small beetle seen from above, as if trapped inside the amber

DO NOT: add text, letters, numbers, labels, a title, a watermark, a frame, a border, a shadow on the background, extra objects, extra gems, or any background scenery. Do not change any gem's shape or colour. Do not reuse emblems from the reference.
