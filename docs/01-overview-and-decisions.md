# 01. Overview, decisions and open questions

## Goals

- Ship two casual Android games from one repo, monetized with ads (interstitial + rewarded), driven by a reusable "ad-brain" that tunes revenue without annoying players.
- Personal side project by Kratos; not related to Motilal Oswal.
- Android first (Google Play). Web build optional for testing.
- General audience, declared 13+.
- Original art, names, sound. No copied IP.

## Stack

- Vite + vanilla JS (canvas) games, wrapped with Capacitor for Android.
- Play Games Services saved games for progress sync.
- GitHub Actions builds a signed `.aab` and uploads to Google Play.
- Ads via AdMob behind an `IAdProvider` interface (Mock first, AdMob later).

## Architecture in one paragraph

Three layers: (1) two games that only emit events (level_complete, level_failed, near_miss, streak_broken, ...) and ask the brain "what should I show?"; (2) `ad-brain`, engine-agnostic, owns all rules, segments, caps, cooldowns, config clamping; (3) adapters: `IAdProvider` (Mock then AdMob), `SaveStore` (Local then Play Games saved games), analytics. Games never import an ad SDK. See docs/02.

## Decisions made (settled)

- Two games, one repo, shared ad-brain and design system.
- One dark theme ("night"), not light/dark switching. Era palettes change backgrounds.
- 10 geological/evolutionary eras x 100 levels = 1000 levels, then endless cycling.
- Levels procedural and seeded (seed = level number); hand-authored only for onboarding (first 10 brick levels, first 15 gem levels).
- History video every 50 levels (2 per era), made from code, skippable, not an ad; unlocks a field-journal entry.
- Interstitials only on happy moments; rewarded offers on need moments; no ads on levels 1-3 or during play.
- Forced-ad probability is never certain (max 0.9) and moves with streaks/fatigue/segment.
- Hard ceilings live in code; remote config is clamped; invalid config falls back to defaults.
- Dino Match has a dig-the-fossil goal (clear dirt over a buried fossil) plus boosters (pick, burst, brush) which can be refilled via a rewarded ad.
- Gem accessibility: every gem has a unique silhouette in addition to colour (diamond, hexagon, rounded square, circle, triangle, star).
- Art for gems/frames/backgrounds is to be generated by Kratos with Gemini from our brief (art/README.md), not copied from a reference game.
- Launch slice proposal: first 4 eras (400 levels), remaining 6 as updates.

## Open decisions (with proposed defaults)

1. **Names.** Working titles Dino Break / Dino Match. Existing apps already use "Dino Match"; run a Play Store + trademark search before art carries a name.
2. **Build order.** Default: design system, then ad-brain with simulator, then brick breaker, then gem match.
3. **Energy/lives in Dino Match v1.** Default: no timer.
4. **Web build** for quick test/share. Default: yes, testing only.
5. **ad-brain numbers** are placeholders; tune in simulator, then on live data.
6. **Mascot.** Default: one invented prehistoric creature drawn from code, used on menus/reward screens in both games.
7. **History video format.** Default: 20 animated videos of 10-20 s made by us, captions, no stock footage, no narrator in v1; every fact checked against a source before scripting.
8. **Launch slice.** Default: 4 eras (400 levels) at launch, one new era per update, cycling after last released era.
9. **Audience risk.** Dinosaurs attract children; declaring 13+ may not hold in Play review. Default: deliberately mature stylized art for teens/adults; read Play Families policy before writing the listing; if Play treats a game as child-appealing, ad settings switch to child-safe (earns less).
