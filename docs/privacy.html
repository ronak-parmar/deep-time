Dino Match – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · App: Dino Match (com.ronki.dinomatch) · Developer: Ronki

Dino Match is a match-3 puzzle game about digging up fossils. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: the game does not ask for your name, contacts, location or photos, and the developer does not run any server that stores your personal data.

1. Game progress

Your progress (levels, stars, fossils, coins, boosters and settings) is saved on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also stored in your Google Play Games "Saved Games" so you can restore it on another device. That copy is stored by Google under your Google account and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. The game reads your Play Games player ID and display name only to show who is signed in and to sync your save.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad for extra moves). AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and app interactions to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Crash and diagnostics

Android and Google Play may collect crash reports and basic diagnostics about the app under Google's own policies. The developer only sees anonymous, aggregated reports.

4. What we do not collect

The developer does not collect or sell your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, files or messages. There are no in-game chats or user accounts run by the developer.

5. Children

Dino Match is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices

  • Play without signing in to Google Play Games: progress then stays only on your device.
  • Delete local progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • Delete cloud saves from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.
  • Change ad consent in the game's settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
